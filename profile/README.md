# EarlyLore

**Research real products that make money before you build.** EarlyLore follows real SaaS products, their revenue evidence, traffic, pricing and positioning changes, and keeps the source and observation date next to every fact.

- 🌐 Website: [earlylore.com](https://earlylore.com) · 中文: [earlylore.com/zh/](https://earlylore.com/zh/)
- 📚 Research scope, method and changelog: [earlylore/docs](https://github.com/earlylore/docs)
- 📈 Traffic data comes from [Vaneform](https://vaneform.com)

**EarlyLore 研究真实在赚钱的产品**：收入证据、流量、定价与变化，每条事实都附来源和观察日期。目前处于研究预览阶段。

<sub>The EarlyLore product source is not open source. This organization hosts public docs only.</sub>
